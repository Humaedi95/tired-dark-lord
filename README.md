## The entry

`main.py` writes a single ~100-word monologue to `/output/entry.txt`, spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice — short sentences, pauses, small deflections ("Not there", "So.", "After you—no, after me") — never in a plain statement of it. The speaker is still a villain with a history and a scheme: a cursed step, a hero buried under the rose beds, and a plan built around the crown, the eclipse, and the seven seals. The last line lands the sting: after three hundred years of waiting, someone still has to lead — and it will have to be him.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (96 words, 503 characters)
```

Deterministic and self-contained: no network access, no dependencies.
